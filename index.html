/ The house

Six bedrooms, six baths, and a pool in the courtyard.

Three thousand nine hundred sixty-one square feet across six bedrooms and six full baths — one each, which is the whole point when two families travel together. One of the six is a bunk room, so the kids sort themselves out.

The courtyard entry opens onto a private pool and spa with a paved sun deck. Upstairs, a covered balcony runs the width of the house off the living room. Tile throughout, which is what you want when everyone is walking in from the beach.

Named for the bird, not the décor. The house itself is white stucco and glass — the pink is our problem, not yours.

Miramar Beach, on the Emerald Coast.

The house sits on Avalon Boulevard in Avalon Beach, Miramar Beach — the stretch of Florida panhandle where the sand is white enough to squeak and the water really is that colour. The Gulf is within a half mile, and you can see it from the upper floor.
