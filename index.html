Built in 2016 and furnished, so you arrive with a suitcase.

Three thousand nine hundred sixty-one square feet across six bedrooms and six full baths — one each, which is the whole point when two families travel together. Tile runs throughout, which is what you want when everyone is walking in from the beach.
